What do they have to offer

Disneyland Park The original Disney park, opened in 1955. It features classic lands like Fantasyland, Adventureland, Tomorrowland, and Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge. Disney California Adventure Park A newer park focused on California-inspired settings and Disney/Pixar stories, featuring Cars Land, Avengers Campus, and Pixar attractions.
