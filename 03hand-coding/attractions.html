Popular Attractions

These are some of the big crowd favorites:

Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance
One of the most immersive and elaborate attractions at Disneyland, making you feel like you are part of a Star Wars adventure.
Space Mountain
An exciting indoor roller coaster that takes you through space in the dark.
Indiana Jones Adventure
A fast-paced adventure ride filled with exciting scenes, obstacles, and special effects.
Pirates of the Caribbean
A classic boat ride that takes you through a pirate-themed adventure.
Big Thunder Mountain Railroad
A fun outdoor roller coaster that races through a western-themed mountain landscape.
Radiator Springs Racers
A popular California Adventure attraction where you race through the world of Cars.
Guardians of the Galaxy - Mission: BREAKOUT!
An exciting drop ride featuring the Guardians of the Galaxy, music, and sudden drops.
Incredicoaster
A thrilling roller coaster featuring the Incredibles, with high speeds, drops, and a loop.
Soarin' Around the World
A gentle flight simulation that gives you the feeling of soaring over famous locations around the world.
Toy Story Midway Mania!
An interactive 3D game ride where you compete in carnival-style games inspired by Toy Story.

Overall, Disneyland and Disney California Adventure offer a wide variety of exciting attractions for visitors of all ages. From thrilling rides like Space Mountain and Incredicoaster to family-friendly favorites like Pirates of the Caribbean and Toy Story Midway Mania, there is something for everyone to enjoy. With so many popular rides and experiences, both parks are great destinations for creating fun memories with family and friends.
